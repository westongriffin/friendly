1. SCREEN RECORDING: attached to the Resolution Center reply (recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS). It shows launch, account creation, creating an event and a group, RSVP, chat, reporting and blocking a comment, polls, the Money tab, and account deletion.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE: Friendly is a private events app for friend groups (adults planning hangouts, dinners, trips, birthdays). It replaces scattered group chats and spreadsheets with one place to plan an event, invite the group, collect RSVPs, chat and share photos on the event's wall, vote in polls, and split the costs afterwards. Everything is invite-only: only the people a host invites can see an event, and only members can see a group.

3. SETUP AND MAIN FEATURES: Sign in with the demo login above. Events tab: tap any event to see the event page (RSVP buttons, +1s, hype reactions, guest list, polls, photos, party wall chat). "+ New event" creates an event: pick a theme and emoji or upload/generate a cover, set date, time, place, and invite group members. Groups tab: "Weekend Crew" shows members, the group chat, group polls, and the crew tab of shared expenses. Money tab: shared expenses, who owes whom, and "Mark as paid"; Venmo and Cash buttons open Venmo or Messages outside the app (no payments are processed in Friendly). Reporting and blocking: on any comment or photo by someone else, tap the flag icon to report it (choose a reason, optionally "also block this person"); reported content is hidden immediately for the reporter. Account deletion: Profile (tap your avatar, top right) → "Delete my account" → enter password. Contacts and notification permissions are optional.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES: Firebase (Authentication for email/password sign-in, Firestore database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging for notifications); Google Vertex AI (Gemini) for optional AI-generated event covers and receipt reading, called only from our servers with the user's prompt or receipt image; Google Places API for address autocomplete and Google Maps for the map preview; Mailgun for sending email invitations with calendar attachments; Venmo and Apple Cash (Messages) are opened as external links only. No payment processor, no ads, no analytics SDKs, no third-party login. The app's screens are delivered from our website officialfriendly.com inside a Capacitor shell with native Contacts and push plugins.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES: none. The app functions identically in all regions. It is available in English only. Address autocomplete is biased toward the United States but works for addresses anywhere.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL: not applicable. Friendly is not in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material. All content is created by users (their own event details, photos, comments) or generated on request by the AI cover feature.
